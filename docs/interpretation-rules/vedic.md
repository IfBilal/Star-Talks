- **Traditional concept:** Natural benefics and malefics
- **Source:** `bphs-sanskritdocuments`, Ch. 3 (ग्रहगुणस्वरूपाध्यायः), verse 11
- **Conditions:** Needs the sidereal planet positions from the chart. Traditional symbolism; not a prediction.

## `vedic.dignity.exaltation`

- **Source:** `bphs-sanskritdocuments`, Ch. 3 (ग्रहगुणस्वरूपाध्यायः), verses 49-50
- **Conditions:** Needs sign and degree of each planet from the chart.

Each planet is at its strongest, exalted, in a particular sign: the Sun in Aries (peak at 10°), Moon in Taurus (3°), Mars in Capricorn (28°), Mercury in Virgo (15°), Jupiter in Cancer (5°), Venus in Pisces (27°), Saturn in Libra (20°). - **Source:** `bphs-sanskritdocuments`, Ch. 3 (ग्रहगुणस्वरूपाध्यायः), verses 59-60
- **Conditions:** Needs dignity of each planet.

The tradition scales a planet's results by its placement: in exaltation the full benefit, in its moolatrikona about three-quarters, in its own sign about half, in a friend's sign about a quarter, in a neutral sign about an eighth, and little or nothing in debilitation, combustion or an enemy's sign. For harmful results the same proportions run in reverse.
