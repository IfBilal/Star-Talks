- **Source:** `di-tian-sui`, 通天論 (opening): the day stem is the heavenly origin (日干為天元)

Saju treats the day stem, called the Ilgan in Korean, as the self at the centre of the chart, with the other seven characters read in relation to it. This is the same concept as the Day Master in Chinese BaZi.
