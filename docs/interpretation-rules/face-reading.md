## `face.forehead.high-or-broad`

- **Topic:** forehead  |  **Readiness:** ready  |  **Testing:** untested  |  **Practitioner review:** not-reviewed
- **Traditional concept:** Forehead that is notably high or notably broad
- **Source:** `coates-faces-1880`, Ch. V 'Signs of Character in the Face', section 'The Forehead' (chapter starts p. 60)
- **Conditions:** Needs a clear, front-facing photo with the feature visible. Symbolic and traditional only; never used to judge character, morality, health, ethnicity, age or attractiveness. Tradition: Western physiognomy, not Chinese Mian Xiang.

In Western physiognomy a high forehead is traditionally linked with breadth of thought and ease in grasping ideas, and a broad forehead with careful attention and accuracy. Offered as a cultural reading, not a measure of ability.
