## `bazi.branch.four-types`

- **Topic:** branches  |  **Readiness:** ready  |  **Testing:** untested  |  **Practitioner review:** not-reviewed
- **Traditional concept:** Birth branches (寅申巳亥), storehouse branches (辰戌丑未) and peak branches (子午卯酉)
- **Source:** `di-tian-sui`, 地支論, third verse
- **Conditions:** Needs the four branches of the chart.

Birth branches are the beginnings of seasons and are read as best left undisturbed; storehouse branches hold stored potential and are read as opened by a clash; peak branches sit at full strength and may welcome either a clash or a combination depending on the chart.

## `bazi.branch.clash`

- **Topic:** branches  |  **Readiness:** ready  |  **Testing:** untested  |  **Practitioner review:** not-reviewed
- **Traditional concept:** Clash (沖) between opposing branches
- **Source:** `di-tian-sui`, 地支論, verses on clashes
- **Conditions:** Needs the four branches of the chart.

A clash is the strongest branch interaction and is read as movement and disturbance. Penalties and harms are weaker and sometimes do not trigger anything, because other combinations may soothe them. A strong branch clashing a weak one pulls it up by the roots, while a weak one clashing a strong one can instead stir it into action.

## `bazi.principle.luck-cycles`

- **Topic:** luck  |  **Readiness:** needs-engine  |  **Testing:** untested  |  **Practitioner review:** not-reviewed
- **Traditional concept:** Ten-year luck cycles and the individual year
- **Source:** `di-tian-sui`, 歲運論 (Years and Luck Cycles)
