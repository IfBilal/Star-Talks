## `palmistry.hand.square`

- **Topic:** hand-shape  |  **Readiness:** ready  |  **Testing:** untested  |  **Practitioner review:** not-reviewed
- **Traditional concept:** Square palm with squared-off fingertips ('useful hand')
- **Source:** `cheiro-hand-1897`, Part I, ch. III 'The Square Hand and its Subdivisions' (chapter starts p. 28)
- **Conditions:** Visible hand or palm photo; no calculation needed.

In traditional palmistry a square palm with squared fingertips points to a practical, orderly temperament: someone who values routine, clear rules and dependable results, works steadily, and prefers methods that have already proved themselves. ## `palmistry.thumb.three-phalanges`

- **Topic:** thumb  |  **Readiness:** ready  |  **Testing:** untested  |  **Practitioner review:** not-reviewed
- **Traditional concept:** The thumb's three sections: tip (will), middle (reason), base (affection)
- **Source:** `cheiro-hand-1897`, Part I, ch. IX 'The Thumb' (chapter starts p. 45)
- **Conditions:** Visible hand or palm photo; no calculation needed.

Palmistry splits the thumb into three parts: the tip stands for willpower, the middle section for reasoning, and the base, which borders the Mount of Venus, for warmth and affection. Whichever section looks longest or fullest shows where the person leans. A strong tip with a short middle is read as acting on will before thinking things through; a long middle with a short tip as sound judgment that can stall without drive.

*Left out on purpose:* Source describes a very long base as sensuality overriding the person; softened to an emphasis on warmth and comfort.
